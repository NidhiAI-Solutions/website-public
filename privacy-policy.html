Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Nidhi AI Solutions Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", "our") operates the Mango Matrix platform, of which LexAI — the mobile app and this website — is one solution. This policy explains what data LexAI collects, how it is used, and the choices and rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

By using LexAI, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Our approach to your data

LexAI is built local-first. Your case data, documents, and notes are stored on your own device and — if you enable backup — in your own cloud account (for example, your iCloud or Google account), not in a Company-controlled database, by default.

Where information does need to reach our backend — for example, to power an AI drafting or research feature — it is tokenised to remove personally identifiable information (PII) before it leaves your device or immediately on receipt, and only that de-identified data is shared with the AI processing partners who power those features.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you register, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and professional details (e.g. bar enrolment information) to create and manage your account and to verify you as an enrolled advocate.

Case and practice data

Client details, case notes, documents, and other practice data you create in the app. This data stays on your device and your own connected cloud account by design, and is not uploaded to our servers except as described in Section 1.

Usage and diagnostic data

Tokenised, de-identified interaction data (e.g. which features are used, crash and performance diagnostics) that helps us operate and improve the app.

Advertising identifiers

Device advertising identifiers and account details described above may be used to show you personalised advertising, as described in Section 5.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your account (application user management).
  • To provide and operate LexAI's features, including AI-assisted drafting and research.
  • To power AI processing through third-party partners, using only tokenised, de-identified data — never your raw case data or client PII.
  • To show personalised advertising using your account details, as described in Section 5.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable law.
  • To improve LexAI based on aggregated, de-identified usage patterns.

4. AI processing and third-party partners

Certain LexAI features rely on AI processing partners. We share only tokenised data with these partners — PII is stripped before any data is sent for processing. Your case data, documents, and client information are never shared with any third party without your explicit permission, and are never sold.

5. Advertising and your choices

Your account/profile details — not your case or client data — may be used to personalise the advertising you see in the app. You can limit ad personalisation at any time through your device's advertising settings, or contact us at support@mangomatrix.ai to opt out.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Case data you have not chosen to back up to your own cloud account exists only on your device and is under your control at all times.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by writing to support@mangomatrix.ai. We will act on verified deletion requests and remove or anonymise the corresponding data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it to meet a legal obligation.

7. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a data principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data, subject to Section 6 above.
  • Withdraw consent for processing at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Register a grievance regarding our handling of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer at support@mangomatrix.ai. We aim to respond to all requests within the timelines prescribed under applicable law.

8. Data security

We use industry-standard measures — including encryption in transit and tokenisation of data before it reaches our backend — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data to industry standards.

9. Children's privacy

LexAI is built for enrolled advocates and legal professionals. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from, children.

10. International data transfers

Some AI processing partners may process tokenised data outside India. Where this happens, we require appropriate safeguards to protect that data, consistent with the DPDP Act.
